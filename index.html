01 / A SIMPLE DAILY HABIT

A moment for you.
Reassurance for them.

Life gets busy. Staying connected can still be simple. Here’s how Daily Ping fits into your day.

  1. 01

    Make your daily ping.

    Open the app and tap Ping to check in. Your home screen shows your last check-in and when an alert will be sent if you don’t ping.

  2. 02

    Choose your people.

    Add the family members, friends, or other trusted people you want to hear from the app when a check-in is overdue.

  3. 03

    Let your safety net know.

    If you miss your check-in, Daily Ping notifies your trusted contacts so they know to reach out to you.

Read our Privacy Policy ↗
What happens if I miss a check-in?

Daily Ping notifies your chosen trusted contacts when your check-in is overdue. The home screen shows the deadline for your next ping.

Who should I add as a trusted contact?

Choose someone you trust to reach out to you, such as a family member or friend. Let them know and get their permission before adding their contact details.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile, go to Settings, and select Delete account. This removes all your Daily Ping account data. If you need help, email support@dailyping.net.
